Descriere Hotel Ibis Barcelona Castelldefels

The ibis hotel in Castelldefels is located 1093 yds. (1000 m) from the beach, 9.3 miles (15 km) from the center of Barcelona, 6.2 miles (10 km) from Barcelona-el Prat Airport and 55.9 miles (90 km) from Port Aventura amusement park. 10 kms to city centre; 14 kms to the airport (barcelona airport);
